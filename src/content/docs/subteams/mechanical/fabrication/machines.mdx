---
title: Machines
---

Fabrication is based on using machines to make parts.

## Subtractive

These machines subtract or take away material to create the desired part.

### Lathe

spinny thing that makes standoffs

### Nebula

thing that cuts polycarb sometimes

### Omio

thing that cuts metal sometimes

### Tormach

thing that cuts metal but cooler

## Additive

These machines add or eject material to create the desired part.

### 3D Printer

thing that spits plastic to make things
