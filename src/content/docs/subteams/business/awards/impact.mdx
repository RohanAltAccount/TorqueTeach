---
title: Impact
---

The Impact Award is one of the most prestigious FIRST awards at competitions, and it's achieved through hard work from our outreach, social, and media teams, during the competition season and in offseason. Our team is generally a top contender for this award every year, but that success is simply a reflection of the determination of all our team members. One of our greatest accomplishments every season is hosting the Remix offseason competition, where we invite teams to compete in the last season's FRC game, and it gathers a substantial amount of donations for nonprofits like Angel Reach.


For the Impact Award, a big part of the award process is our group of Impact presenters. They're responsible for communicating our team’s achievements and values in an engaging, professional, and concise way. Presenters develop strong public speaking and collaborative skills, while representing the heart of our organization to judges and the larger FIRST community.
