title: Social Media

---

Our Social Media team works on maintaining a constant social presence to spread awareness about our organization, and introducing robotics to more people. This is accomplished by actively publishing informational and engaging content across all platforms, such as competition updates, team announcements, outreach events, and more. 

# Member Roles
Our social media team is mainly composed of editors and media members. The social media team works closely with the outreach team to ensure that information about outreach events is properly distributed. The editors work on making content that is informative, engaging, or educational. They usually work with short-form content, such as Instagram Reels. The media team typically films the needed footage for social media posts, like robot pictures, event videos, and team photos, which is then sent to the editors. The editors will compile it into posts or videos and publish it to the platforms. Media team members will be experienced in photography, or may build those skills with practice and help from team members. Editors must know how to use editing software (ex. Davinci Resolve) and must be able to consistently manage social accounts for the organization.
